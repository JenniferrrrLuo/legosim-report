Both versions build the same 18-part model with the same gravity and camera rig. Default follows the authored hand and support choices. Calibrated uses the experimental aria_attachment_v2 profile to vary hand use, build support and timing where the author allows it.

The calibrated builder held the receiving assembly for 27 of 39 evaluation attachments. The default held it for 12 of 39. Median contact-to-release time was 2.7 s with calibration and 0.5 s by default. The real Aria summaries report 92.0% held and 2.6 s to release.

What remains open

The practical test is whether a viewer can identify the picked part, localize its attachment and see the changed build after release. The signed held-out review evaluated 9 of 9 registered pairs. Signed evaluation and cue-by-cue ratings.

The real Aria measurements summarize two previously studied wearers. B built the 18-part prefix. Pooled figures also include later bags. The published charts and tables provide medians, proportions and visibility readouts. The available real data do not supply paired observations for fitting support–posture relationships or uncertainty intervals for the pooled summaries.
